## Film rules
- 2026-11-12 (Merijn: "het relevante onderdeel highlighten, wit / lichtblauw, dan weet de leerling waar hij moet kijken"): every sentence gets a focus highlight. Each beat names the part(s) it explains with `focus: 'key'` or `focus: ['a', 'b']` (keys of the stage's `data-cue` groups), or `focus: null` for an overview sentence; a term's cue joins the beat's focus. While the beat is spoken and its caption shows, the player lights those parts white / light blue with a soft halo (`--fp-focus`, `--fp-focus-glow`, `--fp-focus-top`, `--fp-focus-shade`, `--fp-focus-text`, light and dark values), eased in over 0.4 s, held still with reduced motion and in render mode. Documented in films/_kit/build-film.mjs.
- New films use `strictFocus: true`: the build then fails on a beat without a focus field, on a legacy `cues` list, on a term in an overview beat, or on a key that is no part of the drawing. Older films print warning counts instead (beats that light nothing; cue keys the drawing does not have). Reference film: eng.switchgear.protection.
- 2026-11-12, legibility measured (the v14 rule "labels on the stage at least 12 px at 390 px", docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-07-v14-explainer-films.md): the 12 px is the rendered height of the text's font size in the lesson view, measured in the app at a 390×844 viewport, deviceScaleFactor 1, as font-size units × the stage SVG's on-screen scale (stage width / 640), not on the film page alone.
