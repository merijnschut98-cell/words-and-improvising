# v15: story-based learning

Asked by Merijn on 2026-11-09: "story based learning where possible. That also helps as it's more interesting."

## Why
A story gives the learner a reason to care, a context to hang terms on, and a memory hook. The lessons already use situations (`said` lines, pushback, plant scenes), but they are loose snapshots: a new plant and a new person in every lesson.

## What
1. **One running story per module (or per topic):** a recurring cast and setting that carries through the four levels. Example for eng.flow: Sanne, a new team leader at a packaging plant, meets a bottleneck at Basis, balances the line at Werkkennis, runs a SMED event at Gevorderd, and defends the business case to the plant manager at Expert. Each lesson opens with the next scene (2–4 sentences), the teach blocks explain what she needs, and the check asks what she should do.
2. **Stakes and a turn:** every scene has a problem that matters to someone (a customer order at risk, a near miss, a sponsor losing faith) and ends with a choice. The recall questions stay as they are (pinned); the story frames them.
3. **Recurring cast across modules where it fits:** for example the grid-operator project (Netbasis, Transformatoren, Schakelinstallaties, Netbeheer, Netplanning) as one long project story, and the Projecten track following one consultant (close to Merijn's own work) from intake to close.
4. **Films tell the same story:** the v14b narration opens with the scene and returns to it at the end ("So what does Sanne do on Monday?").
5. **Speaking:** exercises can use the same characters (pitching to the sponsor from the Projecten story).

## Rules
- Facts and numbers stay sourced and asserted; the story never bends them.
- Dutch names and Dutch workplaces; varied genders and roles; no stereotypes.
- Short: a scene is at most about 60 words; the lesson stays within its word limits.
- The story is optional context, not required to answer the questions (a learner who skips it can still pass).

## Order
After the voice rework (v14 first priority) and the remaining v13 part 5 rebuilds. Pilot on one module (eng.flow or pm.intake), Merijn reads it, then roll out per track with a review agent per module (story coherence + facts).

## Checks
- A test that each module with a `story` meta has one scene per lesson, cast names consistent across its lessons, and scene length within the limit.
- The review brief gains a "story" section: coherent arc over the four levels, stakes, no fact bent for the plot.
