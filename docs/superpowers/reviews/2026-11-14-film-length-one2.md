# Review batch one2: length versus information (12 single-lesson films)

Method note. Beat classes are judged against the lesson text in each dossier, strictly: anything that goes beyond the lesson counts as ELABORATION-DECOR unless it makes an abstract point concrete, prevents a misunderstanding, or is needed to carry the worked example. Percentages are approximate and by narration words. Proposed seconds = current rendered seconds minus (cut words / 2.5); no scene count was reduced, so no 2 s per-scene saving is claimed. Cuts are listed by scene.beat; "part" means a sentence inside a beat. For pm.itproject.cutover, pm.lead.difficult, pm.run.conflict and eng.safety.loto the dossier's lesson text is the same as the film's source, but the worked example in several dossiers stops at the question; where the film continues with the answer (cutover scenes 7-8, difficult scenes 6-7, conflict scene 5) I treated that as the example's answer (NEEDED), which I could not check against the full lesson.
