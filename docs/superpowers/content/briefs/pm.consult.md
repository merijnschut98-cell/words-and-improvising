# pm.consult — The consulting craft (projecten, track project, level 4, FADED examples)
Index title: en 'The consulting craft' / nl 'Het adviesvak'. Blurb: en 'Expert level: proposals, pricing, the trusted-advisor relationship, ethics and growing an account.' / nl 'Expertniveau: offertes, prijs, de relatie als vertrouwde adviseur, ethiek en een klant laten groeien.'
1. `pm.consult.proposal` — writing a proposal that wins: the client’s problem first, approach, phasing, what you need from them.
2. `pm.consult.pricing` — pricing: day rates vs fixed price vs value-based; protecting scope; discounts and what they signal.
3. `pm.consult.trust` — the trusted-advisor relationship: credibility, reliability, intimacy, low self-orientation; how it is built.
4. `pm.consult.independence` — independence and ethics: telling the client what they don’t want to hear, conflicts of interest, confidentiality.
5. `pm.consult.account` — growing an account without selling: follow-up, adjacent problems, being asked back.
6. `pm.consult.practice` — building your own practice: positioning, case histories, a network, saying no to the wrong work.
