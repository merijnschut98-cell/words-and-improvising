# pm.consult — The consulting craft (projecten, track project, level 4, FADED examples)
Index title: en 'The consulting craft' / nl 'Het adviesvak'. Blurb: en 'Expert level: proposals, pricing, the trusted-advisor relationship, ethics and growing an account.' / nl 'Expertniveau: offertes, prijs, de relatie als vertrouwde adviseur, ethiek en een klant laten groeien.'
1. `pm.consult.proposal` — writing a proposal that wins: the client’s problem first, approach, phasing, what you need from them.
2. `pm.consult.pricing` — pricing: day rates vs fixed price vs value-based; protecting scope; discounts and what they signal.
3. `pm.consult.trust` — the trusted-advisor relationship: credibility, reliability, intimacy, low self-orientation; how it is built.
4. `pm.consult.independence` — independence and ethics: telling the client what they don’t want to hear, conflicts of interest, confidentiality.
5. `pm.consult.account` — growing an account without selling: follow-up, adjacent problems, being asked back.
6. `pm.consult.practice` — building your own practice: positioning, case histories, a network, saying no to the wrong work.

Facts and sources (checked 2026-11-08):
- Weiss, A., Value-Based Fees (Pfeiffer/Wiley 2002; 3rd ed. Wiley 2021). The “at least a 10:1 return on the buyer’s investment” guideline is from Weiss’s own newsletter, https://alanweiss.com/million-dollar-consulting-mindset-newsletter/2011-02.php. Taught as his rule of thumb, not a market norm; ROI is taught as a conservative estimate of one year’s value divided by the fee (Weiss himself does not tie it to “the first year”; the recall question states the one-year assumption).
- Critique of value and outcome pricing (attribution, timing, several vendors on one number, commoditised work bought on rate): practitioner sources, e.g. https://www.getmonetizely.com/monetizing-agentic-ai-book-saas/four-constraints-on-outcome-based-pricing, https://www.firmsconsulting.com/quarterly/value-based-fees-consulting, https://saxifrage.xyz/post/value-based-pricing. Taught as “the critics have a point”, no statistics.
- Dutch payment terms: statutory 30 days between companies when nothing is agreed; a large company must pay an SME within 30 days, a longer agreed term is void (Art. 6:119a lid 2, 5 and 6 BW, checked on wetten.overheid.nl; Wet van 30 maart 2022, Stb. 2022, 146, in force 1 July 2022) — https://www.rendement.nl/facturen/tools/de-wettelijke-betalingstermijnen-voor-facturen.html, https://www.sra.nl/nieuws/600041/2022/04/grootbedrijf-moet-factuur-mkb-binnen-30-dagen-betalen, https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/nieuws/verkorten-betaaltermijnen-aan-zzp-en-mkb-gaat-1-juli-2022. Start date 1 July 2022 now taught.
- Block, P., Flawless Consulting (1st ed. 1981; 4th ed. Wiley 2023): roles expert / pair of hands / collaborative; resistance: identify the form, name it in neutral language, be quiet; compliance taught as “one of the hardest to see” (no source found for “the hardest”) — https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/peter-block-flawless/9780787948030/ch09.html, https://designedlearning.com/tag/client-resistance.
- Schein, E. H., Helping (Berrett-Koehler 2009): expert, doctor, process consultant; start in the process mode.
- Schein, E. H., Humble Consulting (Berrett-Koehler 2016): messes, Level 1 vs Level 2 relationships, personization, adaptive moves, the attitude of commitment (to help), curiosity and caring (the review’s “3Cs”; the earlier “accessible” was not found) — reviews at https://kimtasso.com/consulting-skills-3-book-review-humble-consulting-by-edgar-h-schein/ and https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/humble-consulting/9781626567221/. The limitation (mainly case stories) is stated as a judgement.
- GDPR Art. 28 processor agreement; personal data includes work data about identifiable people. WOR Art. 27(1)(k) (rules on processing personnel data) and (l) (systems aimed at or suitable for monitoring attendance, behaviour or performance) — https://www.rendement.nl/or-en-digitale-veiligheid/nieuws/welke-invloed-heeft-or-op-de-privacy-van-werknemers.html, https://www.aiactblog.nl/nl/posts/or-instemmingsrecht-ai-op-de-werkvloer. Taught as “may need consent; ask HR”.
- Ooa (Orde van organisatiekundigen en -adviseurs): code of conduct and disciplinary rules (revised with ROA in 2007, https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2007/12/gedragscode-organisatieadviseurs-aangescherpt/); the CMC title is offered through the Ooa and certified by CIIO (https://ooa.nl/professionaliseren/cmc/; Ooa is the Dutch CMC-Global institute, https://www.cmc-global.org/node/190); intervisie is promoted on ooa.nl. The current code text was not retrieved; the lesson tells the learner to check ooa.nl. Management consultant is not a protected title in Dutch law (unlike advocaat or registeraccountant).
- Liability cap “often the fee of the assignment”: common in Dutch general terms; taught with “often”.
- Maister, D., Managing the Professional Service Firm (Free Press 1993): brains / grey hair / procedure, leverage; generosity is taught as common advice “following writers such as Maister” (no direct Maister source found for the word). Client concentration thresholds (a third, a half) are practitioner rules of thumb, hedged.
- Wet DBA: enforcement moratorium lifted on 1 January 2025 — https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2024/9/belastingdienst-gaat-vanaf-2025-weer-handhaven-op-schijnzelfstandigheid/, https://www.zzp-nederland.nl/nieuws/2025-geen-boetes-bij-handhaving-schijnzelfstandigheid-wet-dba. Fines are being phased in (only for intent or gross negligence in 2026; normal enforcement planned from 2027): https://www.mkbservicedesk.nl/nieuws/ondernemersnieuws/handhaving-schijnzelfstandigheid-in-2026-dit-is-wat-er-verandert-en-wat-niet. Taught as “resumed on 1 January 2025, fines phased in; check the current rules with a tax adviser”.
