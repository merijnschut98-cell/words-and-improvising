# pm.close — Close (projecten, track project, level 2)
Index title: en 'Close' / nl 'Afsluiten'. Blurb: en 'Finish well: prove results, close loose ends, learn, and leave the client stronger.' / nl 'Goed afronden: resultaat aantonen, losse eindjes sluiten, leren, en de klant sterker achterlaten.'
1. `pm.close.results` — proving results: baseline vs after, separating your effect from noise, money in the client’s terms.
2. `pm.close.acceptance` — formal acceptance: criteria agreed at the start, sign-off, open points list.
3. `pm.close.retro` — retrospective/evaluation that people learn from: format, safety, turning lessons into actions.
4. `pm.close.report` — the closing report and presentation: one page, the story, what’s next.
5. `pm.close.followup` — follow-up and the next project: account management without being pushy.
6. `pm.close.yourself` — your own reflection: what you’d do differently, collecting feedback, building your case history.
