# pm.lead — Leading people (projecten, track project, level 3, FADED examples)
Index title: en 'Leading people' / nl 'Mensen leiden'. Blurb: en 'Advanced soft skills: influence without authority, feedback, difficult conversations and team dynamics.' / nl 'Gevorderde soft skills: invloed zonder macht, feedback, moeilijke gesprekken en teamdynamiek.'
1. `pm.lead.influence` — influence without authority: interests, reciprocity, credibility, allies; mapping who to win first.
2. `pm.lead.feedback` — giving feedback that lands: SBI, timing, upward feedback to a senior client.
3. `pm.lead.difficult` — difficult conversations: preparing, opening, staying with facts and feelings, ending with an agreement.
4. `pm.lead.teams` — team dynamics: trust, conflict, commitment; spotting what blocks a team and what to do as an outsider.
5. `pm.lead.listen` — listening as a leadership tool: MORE PIES under pressure, silence, questions that open up.
6. `pm.lead.self` — leading yourself: confidence when you are the youngest in the room, handling nerves, recovering from a bad meeting.

### Facts and sources (v13)

- Cialdini, Influence (1984), six principles; Pre-Suasion (2016) adds unity. Goldstein, Cialdini & Griskevicius (2008), J.
