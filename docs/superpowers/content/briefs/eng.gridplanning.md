# eng.gridplanning — Grid planning and the energy transition (engineering, track grid, level 4, FADED examples)
Index title: en 'Grid planning and the transition' / nl 'Netplanning en de energietransitie'. Blurb: en 'Expert level: load flow, N-1, flexibility, batteries and how a grid operator decides what to build.' / nl 'Expertniveau: loadflow, N-1, flexibiliteit, batterijen en hoe een netbeheerder kiest wat er gebouwd wordt.'
1. `eng.gridplanning.loadflow` — load flow in words: voltage drop, loading, why a cable can be full while the station has room.
2. `eng.gridplanning.redundancy` — N-1 and redundancy: what it guarantees, what it costs, when operators relax it.
3. `eng.gridplanning.flex` — flexibility: curtailment, non-firm contracts, time-dependent capacity, congestion management in practice.
4. `eng.gridplanning.storage` — batteries and storage on the grid: where they help, where they make congestion worse.
5. `eng.gridplanning.investment` — investment planning: scenarios, prioritising expansions, lead times of substations and cables.
6. `eng.gridplanning.execution` — from plan to built grid: capacity of the work chain, standardisation, contractors, permits; where improvement work helps most.
