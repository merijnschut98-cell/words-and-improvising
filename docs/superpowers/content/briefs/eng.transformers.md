# eng.transformers — Transformers, cables and connections (engineering, track grid, level 2)
Index title: en 'Transformers and cables' / nl 'Transformatoren en kabels'. Blurb: en 'How transformers and cables work, how they age and fail, and what a new connection really takes.' / nl 'Hoe transformatoren en kabels werken, hoe ze verouderen en falen, en wat een nieuwe aansluiting echt vraagt.'
1. `eng.transformers.principle` — turns ratio, rated power (kVA/MVA), losses, cooling (ONAN), tap changer.
2. `eng.transformers.ageing` — loading and temperature, insulation ageing, oil analysis (DGA) as a health check.
3. `eng.transformers.cables` — MS and LS cable types (XLPE, paper-insulated), joints and terminations, typical failure causes.
4. `eng.transformers.faults` — cable faults: digging damage, joint failures; locating faults; repair time.
5. `eng.transformers.connections` — connection process: request, capacity check, design, work preparation, execution; lead times.
6. `eng.transformers.standard` — standardisation: standard substations and connection types, why standards speed up the work.
