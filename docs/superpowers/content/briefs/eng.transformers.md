Facts and sources added or checked
- Three-phase current I = S / (√3 × U): 630 kVA → 909 A at 400 V, 36.4 A at 10 kV; 400 kVA → 577 A at 400 V, 23.1 A at 10 kV.
- Turns ratio vs voltage ratio: a Dyn5 transformer (HV delta, LV star with neutral) has turns per winding 10,000 / (400/√3) = 43.3; the rated voltage ratio is 25. Recall r2 now states the assumption that both windings are connected the same way (turns ratio = voltage ratio = 50).
- Losses: EU ecodesign regulation 548/2014 (amended 2019/1783), Tier 2 since July 2021, 630 kVA ≤ 24 kV: Pk ≤ 5,400 W, P0 ≤ 540 W (A0 − 10%) → “roughly 0.5 kW no-load, about 5 kW at full load”; efficiency at rated load, pf 1: 630 / (630 + 5.9) ≈ 99.1%. Hum at 100 Hz: magnetostriction at twice the 50 Hz grid frequency.
- Tap changer: off-circuit taps usually ±2 × 2.5% (5 positions); one step = 2.5% × 230 V = 5.75 V ≈ 6 V. Voltage limit 230 V + 10% = 253 V (EN 50160 / Netcode).
- Ageing: IEC 60076-7 (loading guide): relative ageing V = 2^((θh − 98)/6) for non-upgraded paper; design hot spot 98 °C; 12 °C → 4×. Life 40–60 years hedged as “can last”. Oil time constant ~180 min (IEC 60076-7 ONAN default; same as the figure).
- DGA gas interpretation: IEC 60599 / IEEE C57.104 (H2 partial discharge, C2H2 arcing, C2H4 thermal, CO paper).
- Cables: PILC (GPLK = gepantserde papier-loodkabel); XLPE in Dutch MV since the 1980s–90s; water treeing in early XLPE. Drum lengths “a few hundred metres up to about a kilometre” (hedged; depends on cable size). 2,400 / 600 = 4 lengths, 3 joints; 3,000 / 500 = 6 lengths, 5 joints.
- Faults: TDR pulse velocity in MV cable ~150–170 m/µs; 160 × 10 / 2 = 800 m. KLIC notification: required before mechanical digging under the WIBON act, maps from the Kadaster (recall r4 sharpened to “mechanical digging”).
- Connections: 3 × 80 A × 230 V = 55.2 kVA. The fixed 18-week term was removed from art. 23 Elektriciteitswet 1998 per 22 February 2025; the Energiewet (in force 1 January 2026, art. 3.38) only asks for a reasonable term. ACM code decision for large connections (Netcode elektriciteit art. 8.13, in force 1 January 2025): 26 weeks low complexity, 52 weeks medium, high complexity no fixed term, plus a capped dynamic waiting time (38 weeks for 2026). The small-connection code decision (12/18/52 weeks, 2023) was annulled by the CBb on 17 September 2024. Source: Loyens & Loeff, “De aansluittermijn: van harde deadline naar redelijke termijn” (2025). Example 2 + 1 + 3 + 8 + 4 = 18 weeks; recall 2 + 3 + 8 + 4 = 17.
- Standardisation: 300 × (40 − 8) = 9,600 h ≈ 6 FTE at ~1,600 productive h/year (assumption now stated); 250 × 30 = 7,500 h. Transformer delivery times of a year or more since ~2021: widely reported (IEA, US DOE/NREL supply-chain reports), hedged as “often”.

Uncertain / simplified: “often 10 to 20” secondary substations per ring, the 120% one-hour example, and the “few hundred kVA” MV threshold are rough practitioner figures, worded as such. “Streng” for an MV feeder is common DSO usage, but some colleagues say “MS-veld” or “uitgaande kabel”.
