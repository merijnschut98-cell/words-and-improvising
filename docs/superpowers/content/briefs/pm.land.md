# pm.land — Land (projecten, track project, level 2)
Index title: en 'Land' / nl 'Landen'. Blurb: en 'Make the change stick: adoption, resistance, training and handing over to the line.' / nl 'De verandering laten beklijven: adoptie, weerstand, training en overdracht aan de lijn.'
1. `pm.land.change` — change management models in practice (Kotter, ADKAR) without the jargon; what actually drives adoption.
2. `pm.land.resistance` — resistance: forms it takes, what it tells you, how to work with it rather than against it.
3. `pm.land.communication` — communicating change: who hears what when, the middle manager as key channel.
4. `pm.land.training` — training and standard work for new processes; on-the-job vs classroom; checking it stuck.
5. `pm.land.handover` — handing over to the line organisation: ownership, KPIs, what the owner must be able to do alone.
6. `pm.land.sustain` — sustaining results: audits, KPI reviews, the 3-month dip, when to step back in.
