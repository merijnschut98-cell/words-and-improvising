# eng.switchgear — Switchgear and protection (engineering, track grid, level 2)
Index title: en 'Switchgear and protection' / nl 'Schakelinstallaties en beveiliging'. Blurb: en 'Breakers, switchgear, relays and selectivity: how the grid protects itself and people.' / nl 'Vermogensschakelaars, installaties, relais en selectiviteit: hoe het net zichzelf en mensen beschermt.'
1. `eng.switchgear.devices` — circuit breaker vs load-break switch vs disconnector vs fuse; what each can interrupt.
2. `eng.switchgear.installations` — MS switchgear (air- and gas-insulated, SF6 and its alternatives), ring main units.
3. `eng.switchgear.protection` — overcurrent and earth-fault protection, relays, settings, CTs and VTs.
4. `eng.switchgear.selectivity` — selectivity: only the nearest breaker trips; time grading; what goes wrong without it.
5. `eng.switchgear.safety` — working safely: the five safety rules (vrijschakelen, vergrendelen, spanningsloos meten, aarden, afschermen), NEN 3140 roles (IV, WV, VP, VOP) and the written aanwijzing (“AVP” is not a NEN 3140 term, so it is not used).
6. `eng.switchgear.automation` — distribution automation: remote switching, fault indicators, self-healing rings.
