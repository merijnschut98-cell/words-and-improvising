# eng.switchgear — Switchgear and protection (engineering, track grid, level 2)
Index title: en 'Switchgear and protection' / nl 'Schakelinstallaties en beveiliging'. Blurb: en 'Breakers, switchgear, relays and selectivity: how the grid protects itself and people.' / nl 'Vermogensschakelaars, installaties, relais en selectiviteit: hoe het net zichzelf en mensen beschermt.'
1. `eng.switchgear.devices` — circuit breaker vs load-break switch vs disconnector vs fuse; what each can interrupt.
2. `eng.switchgear.installations` — MS switchgear (air- and gas-insulated, SF6 and its alternatives), ring main units.
3. `eng.switchgear.protection` — overcurrent and earth-fault protection, relays, settings, CTs and VTs.
4. `eng.switchgear.selectivity` — selectivity: only the nearest breaker trips; time grading; what goes wrong without it.
5. `eng.switchgear.safety` — working safely: the five safety rules (vrijschakelen, vergrendelen, spanningsloos meten, aarden, afschermen), NEN 3140 roles (IV, WV, VP, VOP) and the written aanwijzing (“AVP” is not a NEN 3140 term, so it is not used).
6. `eng.switchgear.automation` — distribution automation: remote switching, fault indicators, self-healing rings.

Facts and sources added or checked
- Fault level: I = S / (√3 × U): 300 MVA at 10 kV → 17.3 kA; MV breaker ratings 16/20/25 kA are IEC 62271-100 standard values (“typically 16 to 25 kA”).
- Device duties: IEC 62271-100 (circuit breakers), -103 (switches: load-break, making capacity onto a fault), -102 (disconnectors and earthing switches), -105 (switch-fuse combinations).
- SF6 GWP100: 23,500 (IPCC AR5, 2013), 24,300 (AR6, 2021); pre-question “about 23,000 times” and the recall’s stated 23,500 kept. 1.5 × 23,500 = 35,250 kg; 30 kg × 23,500 = 705 t. Leak rate ≤ 0.1%/year: IEC 62271-1 sealed-pressure-system expectation. EU F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573: new MV switchgear ≤ 24 kV without F-gases from 1 January 2026, 24–52 kV from 1 January 2030 (exemptions possible when no alternative is available). Magnefix: solid (resin) insulated MV switchgear widely used in Dutch secondary substations.
- Protection: CT secondary 1 or 5 A, VT secondary ~100 V (IEC 61869). 600/400 = 1.5 A; 450/400 = 1.125 A. Dutch MV networks use resistance, resonant (Petersen coil) or isolated star-point earthing, hence directional/sensitive earth-fault relays. Inverter-based generation limits its fault current to roughly 1–1.5 × rated and can “blind” upstream overcurrent relays: hedged as “can”.
- Selectivity: grading margin 0.3–0.4 s (typical IEC/IEEE practice; the figure `eng.switchgear.selectivity` uses a 0.4 s step and states “typical margin 0.3–0.4 s”, so the text now says 0.3 to 0.4 s; the recall keeps its stated 0.3 s assumption: 0.3 + 0.3 + 0.3 = 0.9 s). gG fuse selectivity ratio 1.6:1 (IEC 60269 rule of thumb), hedged. Figure numbers (relays 0.9/0.5/0.1 s, 11.8/8.4/6.1 kA, 60 ms break time) not restated.
- Safety: the five rules in NEN-EN 50110-1 order (disconnect completely; secure against reconnection; verify absence of voltage; earth and short-circuit; protect against adjacent live parts); verify dead with a tester checked on a live source, before earthing (earthing a live part causes a short circuit and arc). NEN 3140 = Dutch supplement to NEN-EN 50110 for LV (≤ 1,000 V AC); NEN 3840 for above 1,000 V; grid operators’ BEI sector rules. Roles: IV (person in control of the installation, EN 50110 “nominated person in control of an electrical installation”), WV (person in control of the work activity), VP (skilled), VOP (instructed), layperson (ordinary person); written appointment (aanwijzing) by the employer for role, work and installations after a competence check. The example keeps the full sequence with IV-approved switching plan and WV release.
- Automation: 1,200 × 90 = 108,000; 1,000 × 3 + 200 × 90 = 21,000 (−81%); 750 × 2 + 150 × 60 = 10,500. Average annual outage duration in NL 18.8 min (2023) and 23.3 min (2024), Netbeheer Nederland, “Betrouwbaarheid van elektriciteitsnetten in Nederland – resultaten 2024” → “around 20 to 25 minutes a year”. ACM’s quality factor (q-factor) in the tariff regulation links part of allowed income to outage minutes.

Uncertain / simplified: “two or three key stations per ring”, “often within a minute” for self-healing, and relay test intervals “every few years” are practitioner figures, worded as such. “Extra rules for some older types” is kept general on purpose (operator-specific).
